## Overview
BoundBot is an AI-driven customer support solution designed for SaaS businesses. This guide is structured to help you build, train, and deploy your first AI assistant with ease.

Before we dive into the setup steps, here is a look at the final workspace dashboard where you will manage your agents and track performance:

![Dashboard Overview](/images/dashboard-overview.png)

## Core Capabilities
In this documentation, we will explore:
* **Project Initialization:** Setting up your business profile and agent identity.
* **Knowledge Training:** Adding FAQs to make your AI assistant smarter.
* **Visual Customization:** Styling the chat widget to match your brand colors and theme.
* **Live Deployment:** Integrating the chat assistant into your production site.

## Prerequisites
Before you begin, ensure you have:
1. **BoundBot Account:** To access the dashboard and configuration settings.
2. **Business Context:** Basic details about your services or products for AI training.
3. **Website Access:** Where you intend to deploy the final chat widget.
