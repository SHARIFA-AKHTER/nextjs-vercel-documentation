## Getting Started

The onboarding process in BoundBot is designed to be quick and intuitive. To begin, you need to define your business identity and configure your primary AI agent.

### Step 1: Business Profile
First, we set up the core business details to give the AI context about your company:
* **Business Name**: `boundbot-sharifa`
* **Industry**: `SaaS` (Software as a Service)

![Business Setup Process](/images/business-setup.png)

---

## Step 2: Creating Your AI Agent

Once the business is registered, you move to the **Create Agent** phase. For our workspace, we built the following:

* **Agent Name**: `b Assistant`
* **Agent Tone**: `Friendly tone` — chosen to ensure a welcoming and helpful customer experience.
* **Welcome Message**: "Hey there! Welcome! How can I help you today?"

![Agent Configuration](/images/agent-config.png)

---

## Step 3: Training the Knowledge Base

A great AI agent needs data to provide accurate answers. We trained **b Assistant** by adding essential FAQs:

| Question | Example Response |
| :--- | :--- |
| What services does BoundBot provide? | BoundBot provides a suite of AI-driven automation tools... |

![FAQ and Knowledge Base Training](/images/faq-setup.png)

---

## Step 4: Verification in Playground

Before moving to customization, it is important to test the agent's behavior. 

1. Use the **Playground** tab to chat with your agent.
2. Verify if the tone matches your brand.
3. Check if the AI captures leads correctly as per the system prompt.

![Final Setup Summary](/images/launch-summary.png)

> **Next Step:** Once your agent is ready, proceed to [Customizing the Chat UI](boundbot-customization) to design your widget's appearance!
